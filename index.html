Shadows of Tholu

Vijayawada International Airport|2026|KMV Projects

A suspended leather canopy of Andhra's shadow puppetry, at Vijayawada International Airport.

An airport becomes a stage

Envisioned as a “sky of shadows,” this leather installation reimagines the arrival experience at Vijayawada International Airport. Suspended above the terminal, it turns a place of transit into a place of story, and gives the city's gateway a sense of place that could belong nowhere else in the world.

Hand-cut and hand-painted in Nimmalakunta, the work brings Tholu Bommalata, the leather shadow puppetry of Andhra Pradesh, out of the village theatre and into one of the state's busiest public spaces. For a craft that has always lived in lamplight, the airport becomes a new stage.

Spanning 5,600 square feet and made up of 2,650 individual leather pieces, it is, to our knowledge, the largest single craft installation in India.

A canopy that filters light like a shadow screen

Tholu Bommalata is performed by passing light through translucent, intricately perforated leather. The installation holds to that principle. Layers of treated leather figures hang at varying heights, so daylight and terminal lighting pass through the perforations and scatter patterned shadows across the floor and the passengers below.

Gods, epics, dancers and the everyday life of the Telugu land are carved into the hides, so travellers walk beneath the stories rather than simply past them.

Hanuman's Journey to the Pooja Room

Sreenivasan, owner of Telugu Titans|2020

A 40 × 8 ft leather puppetry ceiling that turns the walkway from the main door to the pooja room into a pilgrimage.

The concept

In this home, the walkway from the main door to the pooja room is more than a passage. Overhead, a story unfolds: Hanuman is travelling to the pooja room, and the whole ceiling is his path. As you walk, you follow him through the world he crosses.

He passes birds in flight and drifting clouds, then mountains and a forest of tall trees. Beyond them lie crop fields, a river, and finally a sea alive with fish. Each landscape is its own zone of the ceiling, so the journey can be read from one end of the walkway to the other.

Translating Craft to Scalable Design

Everything is made from natural leather, hand-drawn and hand-cut in the Tholu Bommalata tradition. Lit from within and around, the translucent hides glow warm gold, and the fine perforations throw delicate shadows onto the walls, just as they do on a puppet theatre screen. The craft that once told these stories by lamplight now does it overhead, in a home.

Indianising The Jungle Book Characters

Disney-USA|2020

A design internship focused on adapting Disney's iconic characters into traditional Indian puppetry aesthetics through color, form, and costume analysis.

Reinterpreting Visual Elements

The objective was to completely reimagine the familiar forms using the intricate visual language of regional Indian puppetry. This required deep cultural research into traditional pigments and fabrics.

Each character design was broken down to its geometric essence, translated into flat, two-dimensional articulated planes that reflect the mechanics of shadow puppets.

A Cultural Narrative Through Character

The resulting project successfully married recognizable pop-culture silhouettes with hyper-local heritage art, acting as a case study for visual localization.


Harmonious was a nighttime spectacular at EPCOT, Walt Disney World Resort, in Bay Lake, Florida, created for Walt Disney World's 50th anniversary celebration in 2021. It invited audiences to travel the globe through new versions of classic Disney songs, reimagined in more than a dozen languages by a diverse group of 240 artists from around the world. I was proud to be one of them, and to bring the craft of Andhra Pradesh to that stage.

Contemporary Home Decor

NIFT Master of Design|2024 - 2026

Translating traditional Indian craft techniques, motifs, and materials into modern interior applications and scalable home decor products.

Bridging Heritage and Modernity

A deep dive into natural dyes, surface detailing, and the integration of wood and leather into minimalist living spaces. The study serves as a bridge for heritage crafts to enter contemporary markets.

The exploration of texture and rhythmic pattern forms the basis of the collection, allowing spatial interiors to benefit from the tactile richness of historically significant weaving techniques.

Material Surface and Experimentation

Through material surface interventions, the final pieces establish a sophisticated dialogue between the raw, imperfect nature of the craft and the refined demands of modern spatial styling.
